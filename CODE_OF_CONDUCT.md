# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone taking part in raindrop — maintainers, contributors, issue reporters,
and passers-by — deserves a respectful, welcoming experience. The project
builds on open, community-driven protocol work, and we hold ourselves to the
same standard as the community it comes from.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being kind, patient, and assuming good faith.
- Offering and accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Explaining the "why" behind a design, not just the "what".
- Respecting accessibility needs and different communication styles.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional or community setting.
- Deliberately introducing malicious or exploitative code into contributions.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository, its issue tracker and PRs, and
in project spaces (e.g. when the maintainer represents the project elsewhere).

## Enforcement

Report instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
contact@ewancroft.uk (or privately to @ewancroft.uk on Bluesky). All reports
are reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that
violate this code of conduct, and will explain the reason when doing so.
